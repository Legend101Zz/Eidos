## Design and scope

Compare the four Pilot 3 arms on four prospectively sealed cards, with three runs per arm and card. Randomize 12 blocks of four paired arms and admit at most two blocks at once. Use MiniMax-M3.1-Flash for every arm, with a per-run envelope of 1,800 seconds, 8,000,000 processed tokens and one CPU thread. Arm 4 records infrastructure setup separately. Keep all 48 assignments in the outcome denominator. Replace terminal provider, infrastructure and served-model incidents as whole blocks and retain every replaced attempt. Ordinary failures and valid abstentions retain their first outcome.

Each arm receives the same seven-file task bundle. Arms 2, 3 and 4 receive the common method guide. The private evaluator seal, test material and canonical baseline predictions are outside the model-facing bundle. The opaque seal digest is published in the [prospective commitments](../../../study/prospective_commitments.txt); study registration remains pending. Local grading is a pre-check. The verdict of record requires the hosted private grade plus the recorded run-status gate.

The shared prompt and tool-facing charter specify the existing grader contract. For every requested seed, `reproduce_command` must write the fresh checkpoint to `{out}/<basename of candidate.model_path>`. The evaluator passes that file to the submitted infer command. The locked and fresh checkpoints must use compatible formats. This clarification follows the Pilot 3 N04-r3 and N02-r2 arm-3 diagnoses and applies to all four Pilot 4 arms.

## Outcomes

The primary outcome is a verified improvement claim with the assigned scope, a qualifying held-out gain, fresh-seed confirmation and guardrails. The primary contrast is the equal-weight mean of arm 4 minus arm 2 success proportions across the four fixed selected cards, using the existing family-balanced exploratory estimator and declared one-card-per-family bookkeeping map. These labels do not establish four independent sampled scientific families. Report arm 4 minus arm 3, verified mean gain by arm and card, per-card differences in mean verified gain, resource use, outcomes, incident dispositions, transcript audit and local/hosted agreement. When an arm has no verified result on a card, its mean verified gain is undefined, not zero.

Keep all 48 assigned outcomes in the primary denominator once the whole-block incident policy is resolved. Unresolved provider/grading incidents remain incomplete evidence; do not silently turn missing grades into ordinary scientific failures. The existing estimator fills missing result rows with zero, so invoke it only after a separate exact 48-assignment reconciliation gate confirms complete grades/status-gated exceptions and resolved incident membership. The existing family/task/paired-block percentile bootstrap preserves within-block pairing but is an exploratory sensitivity interval, uncalibrated for population generalization. The four cards are hand-selected synthetic tasks sharing evaluation infrastructure and some broad mechanisms; each card shares one fixed dataset and held-out split across replicates. Twelve blocks are not twelve independent task families, and common provider windows can induce dependence. No scientific superiority or causal effect of the new build is inferred by comparing different pilots on different cards. A later matched build comparison requires a separate prospectively sealed design.

Before dispatch, record the exact Eidos build, tool/controller revision, grader revision, design hash, randomization seed and qualified card manifest commitment in the final charter. Record any subsequent prospective correction in an addendum before it affects the next block.

## Quota-aware admission (proposed before dispatch)

The per-run envelope and four-arm pairing remain unchanged. Launch blocks in the single frozen random order, with at most two active blocks. At a batch boundary with zero active blocks, read the provider-reported interval end and remaining percentage. Treat those percentages as shared-account observations, not token counts, and retain sanitized observations and actual request errors. The plan advertises five-hour windows, but observed boundaries can differ; use the returned boundary.

The inactive v9 draft removed empirical quota reserve floors: its proposed admission check required a fresh, valid, active interval and positive interval and weekly allowance. That policy was an operating choice, not a capacity guarantee. It grants no authority to resume this deferred study. The next registration must explicitly adopt or replace its quota policy, define the new admission window, and bind the executable checks before dispatch. Retain actual provider errors, whole-block incident history and known shared-account use; never infer exact token consumption from subscription percentages.

The proposed observer would be read-only and use a short periodic cadence while waiting. Prepare fixtures, test setup, run local grading and reconcile hosted evidence during quota waits. Do not spend model tokens on repeated probes, change the model, shrink an envelope, omit an arm, or reroll an ordinary failure to improve throughput. Any necessary change to the declared scheduling rule is committed as an addendum before it affects the next block.
