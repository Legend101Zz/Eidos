# Improvement programme standing charter

Development evidence only; no confirmatory certification or release decision.
Each investigation receives 1,800 seconds, 8,000,000 processed tokens and one CPU
thread. Select arm 4 or the paired arms 2 and 4 prospectively. Baseline stubs are
mechanical controls; fixture autopilot runs are model-free development evidence.
The controller records treatment, provider, build, card roster and block order
before running. Independent grading uses the registered practical bar, held-out
predictions, artifact checks and fresh-seed reproduction. Incident retries replace
whole blocks and preserve the previous evidence. Honest nulls and abstentions are
allowed; false improvement claims are counted. Measurement and decisions follow
experiments/rules/; paired runner summaries are descriptive and make no decision.
The blind H consultation has a caller-pinned threshold and at most three looks.
This file binds controller provenance and supplies no additional model prompt.
